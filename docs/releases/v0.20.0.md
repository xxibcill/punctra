## Exact browser qualification

The full packed browser harness passed the one checked-in runtime lane. It
loaded the 70,000-Point immutable Source, displayed its 4,096-Point sampled
root in four batches, exercised every accepted lifecycle, input, presentation,
exactness, recovery, latency, and independent resource gate, and published
`punctra-browser-qualification-v1`.

The non-standard `performance.memory.usedJSHeapSize` observation had a
8,350,236-byte phase high-water. Process RSS, physical cache allocation, and
physical GPU/driver allocation were not available and remain explicit nulls.
The timing endpoints are main-thread submission facts, not physical GPU
completion or display-panel presentation.

Pre-publication Worker and network failures retained the viewer and generation;
retry succeeded after correction. Cancellation retained the last viewer/frame.
Over-limit resize retained the prior viewport. Partial publication and device
loss remain recreation-required and are covered deterministically rather than
forced destructively on the physical adapter. Stale generations were rejected.
