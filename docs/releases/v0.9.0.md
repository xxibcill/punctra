There is no version-2 persisted format and no migration framework in v0.9.
Authoritative version-1 bytes remain readable and fail closed on unknown
versions. The version-1 index is rebuildable under the explicit policy in the
[support matrix](../architecture/v0.9-support-matrix.md). Temporary files have
no cross-release readability promise.

The checked-in manifests are owned locally by `point-source`, `source-memory`,
`source-las`, `point-index`, `point-workspace`, and
`terrain-demo/tests/fixtures/qualification-v1`. They pin relative paths,
support classes, lengths, BLAKE3 hashes, versions, identities, and semantic
facts. Authoritative fixtures reopen without changing checked-in bytes; index
fixtures exercise open/resume/rebuild policy; Run prefixes, report, LandXML,
and canonical pass/fail evidence match their manifest.
